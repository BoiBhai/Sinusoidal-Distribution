\newline{\Large\textbf{Conclusion}}
\vspace*{0.5cm}\\
In the current iteration, we saw the applicability of the \sindist{} to real-world problems -- data fitting, test-statistic modelling, regression and as neural network activation functions. We conclude that the \sindist{} is a highly potent distribution which can find application in a wide variety of statistical tasks once we leverage its flexibility that comes at the cost of its computational complexity.
